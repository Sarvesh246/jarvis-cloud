Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Vaelo is a personal AI assistant used only by its developer. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

Vaelo uses Google sign-in to verify the account owner's identity. It receives a Google account identifier, verified email address and name. The separate local assistant, Jarvis, has its own Google Workspace integrations and credentials; those are not connected to this cloud website.

How data is used

Sign-in information is used to restrict access to the account owner and identify their workspace. Google identity tokens are verified by the server and are not sent to model providers.

Storage and sharing

The private website uses Google sign-in to verify the owner's identity. It stores a signed session in a secure, HTTP-only cookie. Sign-in does not grant the cloud website access to Gmail, Drive or other Google Workspace APIs.

Website conversations are saved in a private Vercel Blob store and cached in the owner's browser for offline reading. Devices signed into the same Google account automatically sync conversations, reply versions, pins and documents. Storage credentials stay on the server; browser requests use the signed-in account and protected write endpoints.

Prompts are sent through Vercel to a separate Hermes deployment on Render. Prompts and requested research are processed by the configured model and search providers. Memory and learned skills on Render's free instance may be lost when it restarts or redeploys; the website's cloud chat history is stored separately. Clearing site data removes the local cache, which is restored from cloud history after sign-in. Deleting a conversation syncs its removal to other devices. Replaced storage chunks are removed after the new version is saved; interrupted uploads and failed cleanup may leave private storage remnants.

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Google user data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, except as needed to process the owner's own requests.

Vaelo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
