Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Jarvis is a personal AI assistant used only by its developer. It is not available to the public.

Data accessed

With the account owner's permission, Jarvis accesses Google user data (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Sheets and Google Docs) through Google APIs.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to carry out tasks the account owner requests, such as reading or sending email, managing calendar events, and working with files. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training AI models.

Storage and sharing

The private website uses Google sign-in to verify the owner's identity. It stores a signed session in a secure, HTTP-only cookie. Sign-in does not grant the cloud website access to Gmail, Drive or other Google Workspace APIs.

Website conversations are saved in the owner's browser and sent through Vercel to a separate Hermes deployment on Render. Prompts and requested research are processed by the configured model and search providers. Conversations, memory and learned skills on Render's free instance may be lost when it restarts or redeploys. Clearing site data removes the browser's saved chats.

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Google user data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties, except as needed to process the owner's own requests.

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
